Arbri Jesku

Arbri Jesku is an Electronics Engineer and researcher with a multidisciplinary scientific profile spanning Remote Sensing, UAV-based systems, sensor technologies, the Internet of Things , Machine Learning. He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Electronics Engineering and is currently pursuing a Master of Science in Electronics Engineering at the Polytechnic University of Tirana. Alongside his academic activities, he works as a Telecommunications Engineer at Tring TV, where he is involved in real-time signal monitoring, broadcast system operation, troubleshooting, and technical infrastructure support. His research interests focus on advanced sensing platforms, UAV-based data acquisition, intelligent monitoring systems, machine-learning-assisted data analysis, and IoT-enabled solutions for engineering, environmental, and technological applications. His professional experience in real-time monitoring and complex technological infrastructures complements his research activities and strengthens his ability to develop reliable and application-oriented engineering solutions.
